# Incumbent piano/music-learning apps and market size (as of October 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-19. Labels used below: **[DISCLOSED]** means the company said it or a statutory filing shows it. **[REPORTED]** means a journalist reported it, often from unnamed sources. **[ESTIMATE]** means a third-party estimate (aggregator, market-research firm or app-intelligence model). Market-research publishers disagree with each other by up to 10x, so cite the publisher and the year every time.

---

## 1. Market size: online music education, music-learning apps, piano apps, demand, instrument sales

### Takeaway
Paid market-research reports put "online music education" at about USD 2–5.6B in 2025, growing at 10–18% a year. One outlier says USD 20B. Piano is the largest instrument segment at roughly 34–39% of revenue. The demand signal is strong: piano is the instrument most would-be learners want to play (about 47% in the US, 48% in the UK). No authoritative bottom-up figure for piano-app revenue exists. The best proxies are the incumbents' own numbers: Simply had about USD 100M of revenue or ARR in 2020, and Yousician had €53M in 2025.

### Cited Findings
**Online music education market (top-down; all [ESTIMATE])**
- Mordor Intelligence: USD 3.9B (2025), USD 4.61B (2026), USD 9.36B by 2031, a 15.23% CAGR. Piano held **38.85%** of revenue in 2025. — [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/online-music-education-market)
- Knowledge Sourcing: USD 2.19B (2025) to USD 4.9B (2030), a 17.52% CAGR. — [Knowledge Sourcing](https://www.knowledge-sourcing.com/report/online-music-education-market)
- Expert Market Research: USD 4.27B (2025) to USD 14.75B by 2035, a 13.2% CAGR. — [Expert Market Research](https://www.expertmarketresearch.com/reports/online-music-education-market)
- Market Research Intellect: USD 5.56B (2025) to USD 15.25B by 2035, a 10.61% CAGR. — [Market Research Intellect](https://www.marketresearchintellect.com/product/online-music-education-market/)
- Global Growth Insights (outlier): USD 20B (2025) to USD 101.39B by 2035. Not credible next to the others. — [Global Growth Insights](https://www.globalgrowthinsights.com/market-reports/online-music-education-market-102191)
- Astute Analytica: piano share 34.2%. Its own figures are internally inconsistent, so treat with caution. — [Astute Analytica](https://www.astuteanalytica.com/industry-report/online-music-education-market)
- Grand View Research (old, secondhand): USD 3.67B in 2019 with an 18.1% CAGR for 2020–2027, with piano as the largest segment. I found only a secondary citation and could not locate a current Grand View page. — [FasterCapital citing Grand View](https://fastercapital.com/content/Piano-learning-app--Marketing-Strategies-for-Piano-Learning-Apps--Captivating-the-Entrepreneurial-Audience.html)
- Other reports (SkyQuest, MetaStat, Marketintelo "Music Learning Apps") exist but were not checked in detail. — [SkyQuest](https://www.skyquestt.com/report/online-music-education-market); [Marketintelo](https://marketintelo.com/report/music-learning-apps-market)

**Bottom-up proxies (incumbent revenue)**
- Simply (JoyTunes): about USD 100M revenue in 2020, up 150% on 2019 and "still believed to not be profitable" (Calcalist, June 2021) [REPORTED]. Globes called the same number USD 100M **ARR**, of which Simply Guitar was USD 10M [REPORTED]. — [CTech/Calcalist, Jun 2021](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3910571,00.html); [Globes, 2021](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-joytunes-raising-50m-at-1b-valuation-1001375306)
- Yousician Oy: revenue €53.3M (2024) and about €53.0M (2025) [DISCLOSED via Finnish statutory accounts, as compiled by registry aggregators]. — [Asiakastieto](https://www.asiakastieto.fi/yritykset/fi/yousician-oy/23743640/yleiskuva); [Finder.fi](https://finder.fi/Sovellukset+ja+ohjelmistot/Yousician+Oy/Helsinki/yhteystiedot/2476700)
- Musora (Drumeo/Pianote etc.): "mid-eight-figure" revenue, profitable, more than 100,000 paid subscribers (Globe and Mail, 2025, as summarised by Upbeat Studio) [REPORTED]. — [Upbeat Studio landscape map, Sep 2026](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/); [Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-how-a-bc-drum-teacher-built-global-online-juggernaut-drumeo)
- Duolingo: its FY2025 annual report says the Music and Math courses "have not generated material revenue". Duolingo had 137.8M MAU and 12.5M paid subscribers in Q1 2026. — [Upbeat Studio, citing Duolingo 10-K and Q1 2026 results](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)

**Demand: people who want to learn piano**
- In a YouGov survey (2022, about 3,000 US adults, per Thomann), Americans who want to learn an instrument chose piano 47%, guitar 22% and drums 8%. About half of Americans who never learned an instrument wish they had. I found only the secondary write-up, not the YouGov original. — [Thomann blog citing YouGov](https://www.thomannmusic.com/blog/inspire/study-more-young-adults-embrace-music/)
- UK (YouGov): 48% of would-be learners would choose the piano. Piano and guitar are the instruments Britons most often say they can play. — [YouGov UK](https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/51268-how-many-britons-can-play-a-musical-instrument)
- YouGov MusicLab, a different survey: 25% of all respondents would most like to learn piano, and 47% of current instrument players play piano. Do not confuse the two 47% figures. — [YouGov US](https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/43512-young-americans-increasingly-exposed-music)
- Upbeat Studio's estimate of beginner demand by instrument: piano 30–45%, guitar 25–40%, drums about 8%. — [Upbeat Studio, Sep 2026](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)

**Keyboard/digital piano sales (all [ESTIMATE], wide disagreement)**
- Digital piano market value in 2024: USD 1.066B (Intel Market Research), USD 1.23B (Business Research Insights), USD 1.89B (pmarketresearch), USD 2.13B (Wiseguy). Dataintelo puts 2025 at USD 2.8B. — [Intel MR](https://www.intelmarketresearch.com/digital-piano-market-28680); [Business Research Insights](https://www.businessresearchinsights.com/market-reports/digital-piano-market-122260); [pmarketresearch](https://pmarketresearch.com/worldwide-digital-piano-market-research/); [Wiseguy](https://www.wiseguyreports.com/reports/digital-piano-market); [Dataintelo](https://dataintelo.com/report/global-digital-piano-market)
- Unit estimates conflict: one report says about 906k digital pianos were consumed globally in 2023, while another implies 9–15M units a year. — [Archive Market Research / Intel MR, as surfaced in search](https://www.archivemarketresearch.com/reports/digital-piano-231387)
- US (weak, unsourced consumer guide): about 188,000 digital pianos sold in the US "last year", against 17,294 acoustic units in 2024. — [unanswered.io](https://unanswered.io/guide/are-pianos-losing-popularity-trends)
- Online channels reportedly handled about 28% of global piano transactions in 2025, up from 19% in 2020 [ESTIMATE]. — [Data Insights Reports](https://www.datainsightsreports.com/reports/global-modern-piano-market-72896)
- Hardware makers bundle learning apps. Yamaha bundles flowkey, with a promotion running to March 2027. Casio built Music Space (2022) and Donner launched Donner Play (2026). — [Upbeat Studio, Sep 2026](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)

### Inferences
- For a pitch, a defensible TAM framing is "online music education at about USD 4–5B in 2025–26 (Mordor, Expert Market Research), with piano at about 35–39%". That gives a piano-specific online-learning SAM of roughly USD 1.4–1.8B. This is derived arithmetic, not a published number.
- Revenue among known apps is concentrated. Simply (≥USD 100M in 2020, probably more now) plus Yousician (€53M) plus Musora (mid-eight figures) together likely make up a large share of the real, observable app-subscription market. That suggests the top-down reports overstate the app-specific opportunity.
- Yousician's revenue has been flat since 2024, which suggests that segment of the market is mature or saturated.

### Gaps
- No Sensor Tower, Appfigures or AppMagic public report on the "music learning" category's total consumer spend was found.
- No NAMM-sourced digital piano unit data for 2024–2025 was found. NAMM Global Report data is members-only.
- Grand View Research's current (2024–2026) figure could not be retrieved. HolonIQ has no public music-edtech sizing that I could find.

## 2. Simply (formerly JoyTunes): Simply Piano, Guitar, Sing, Draw

### Takeaway
Simply is the category leader: Israeli, valued at USD 1B in 2021, with about USD 97M raised. It reported about USD 100M of 2020 revenue (or ARR) and has disclosed nothing newer. Since then it has broadened into "creative hobbies" (Draw, Sing) and XR/glasses platforms rather than raising capital. Its model is a heavily paid-acquisition-driven subscription with constant paywall and pricing testing.

### Cited Findings
**Corporate and funding**
- Founded by Yuval Kaminka (CEO), Yigal Kaminka and Roey Izkovsky. Headquartered in Tel Aviv. Rebranded from JoyTunes to Simply in July 2022. Its patented note-recognition engine is called "MusicSense". — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_(software_company))
- Funding history: Series A of USD 5M (Aug 2014), led by Aleph with Formation 8 and Genesis Partners. Then USD 25M at the end of 2019, led by Qumra Capital with existing investor **Insight Partners**. Then USD 50M in June 2021, led by GV with Qualcomm Ventures and Hearst Ventures, at a **USD 1B valuation**. Wikipedia gives total raised as about USD 97M; Tracxn gives USD 93M over 6 rounds. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_(software_company)); [CTech, Jun 2021](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3910571,00.html); [NoCamels, Jun 2021](https://nocamels.com/2021/06/joytunes-unicorn-music-education-app/); [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/music-app-maker-joytunes-said-to-be-new-israeli-unicorn-with-google-investment/); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/simply/__T8RJAYJclzBT1-gnDo9AmQmccws8X8fh5Lm6aVhJ__g)
- CB Insights lists the June 2021 Series D (USD 50M) as the last round. I found no newer round through October 2026. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/deal/joytunes-other-investors)

**Revenue (disclosed or reported vs estimated)**
- Late 2019 [DISCLOSED by co-founder]: more than 200,000 paying subscribers and more than USD 20M in annual revenue. — [CTech, 2019](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3769058,00.html)
- 2020 [REPORTED]: about USD 100M revenue (up 150%, a COVID boost) and not profitable, per Calcalist. Globes called it USD 100M ARR. — [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3910571,00.html); [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-joytunes-raising-50m-at-1b-valuation-1001375306)
- 2024 [ESTIMATE]: GetLatka says about USD 200M ARR. Owler gives a band of USD 100–500M with 100–250 employees. Growjo says USD 34.6–38.7M and Datanyze says USD 42M; both are model outputs and likely stale. — [GetLatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/simply-); [Owler](https://www.owler.com/company/hellosimply); [Growjo](https://growjo.com/company/Simply_(formerly_JoyTunes)); [Datanyze](https://www.datanyze.com/companies/simply/1325825114)
- Apple's Search Ads case study describes JoyTunes as having grown "into a profitable business". This is self-described and undated, with no figures. — [Apple Ads](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/success-stories/joytunes)
- Sensor Tower [ESTIMATE, single snapshots, month not verifiable]: Simply Piano on the US App Store was about 400k downloads and about USD 5M revenue in one month. On US Google Play it was about 800k downloads and about USD 1M in one month. — [Sensor Tower iOS](https://app.sensortower.com/overview/1019442026?country=US); [Sensor Tower Google Play](https://app.sensortower.com/overview/com.joytunes.simplypiano?country=US)

**Scale**
- Simply Piano has more than 50M Google Play installs, and Simply Guitar has more than 10M (Aug 2026). — [Upbeat Studio, Sep 2026](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)

**Products and recent news, 2023–2026**
- Portfolio: Simply Piano (2017), Simply Guitar, Simply Sing (AI pitch feedback), Simply Draw (2023, AI feedback on drawings) and Simply Tune (free tuner). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_(software_company))
- 2024: partnership with Trinity College London, so certain courses lead to a TCL certificate. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_(software_company))
- December 2024: Simply Piano on Apple Vision Pro, with a virtual keyboard and no physical keyboard needed. — [9to5Mac via archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20250528033726/https://9to5mac.com/2024/12/03/simply-piano-now-lets-you-learn-to-play-through-apple-vision-pro-no-keyboard-required/)
- Simply Piano for Android XR (PR Newswire, dated January 2026 in one listing; CB Insights says August 2026). — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/simply-expands-its-creative-hobbies-vision-with-the-launch-of-simply-piano-for-android-xr-302653709.html); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/joytunes)
- April 2026: a Benzinga headline says Simply supports Meta AI glasses (headline only; not verified). — [Owler news feed](https://www.owler.com/company/hellosimply)
- July 2026: Oxford University Press content added to Simply Piano (CB Insights news item). — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/joytunes)
- No layoff reports for 2024–2026 were found in searches.

**Pricing and business model**
- Simply Piano pricing varies by region and test cell. Reviews cite USD 17.90/month or USD 169.90/year (Individual), while others cite USD 119–150/year or up to USD 224.99/year (family). — [pianoers.com review, 2026](https://pianoers.com/simply-piano-review-the-honest-truth-about-learning-piano-with-an-app/); [Skoove comparison, 2026](https://www.skoove.com/blog/best-piano-apps/)
- The CEO wrote that a single localized pricing test lifted worldwide revenue 15%, and that Simply runs "hundreds of tests". — [Medium / hellosimply](https://medium.com/hellosimply/how-big-macs-increased-our-revenue-by-15-71855cda055a)
- A job posting for a "Performance Video Creator" to make large-scale video ads for growth shows reliance on paid social UA. It calls Simply Piano, Guitar, Sing and Draw "top-grossing apps". — [BuiltIn job posting](https://builtin.com/job/marketing-video-creator/3795861)
- Apple Search Ads is a documented acquisition channel (Apple success story). — [Apple Ads](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/success-stories/joytunes)

### Inferences
- With no round since 2021 and a USD 1B mark set at the COVID peak, Simply is either self-funding (consistent with Apple's "profitable" claim) or has a stale valuation. Either way it is unlikely to be a new-money competitor. Its moves into XR and drawing suggest it is looking for growth beyond piano.
- If the Sensor Tower iOS-US snapshot (about USD 5M/month) is representative, US iOS alone would be about USD 60M a year. That is consistent with a total business well above USD 100M. This is an inference only.

### Gaps
- No disclosed revenue after 2020, no current subscriber count, and no official MAU figure.
- No disclosed UA spend or Meta/TikTok spend figures. No public Sensor Tower ad-intelligence post was found.
- Simply Drum was not confirmed as a product.

## 3. Yousician

### Takeaway
Yousician (Helsinki) is the best-documented incumbent because Finnish accounts are public. It had €53.3M revenue in 2024 and about €53.0M in 2025, so growth is flat. It swung from an operating loss in 2022 (−€8.1M) to a small profit in 2025 (about €3.5–3.7M, a 6.5% margin). It has raised about €35M in total, none since its 2021 Series B, and runs a freemium funnel through the GuitarTuna tuner app.

### Cited Findings
- Founded December 2010 as Ovelin by Chris Thür (CEO) and Mikko Kaipainen. Covers guitar, piano, ukulele, bass and voice. Lessons are produced in-house. Freemium model: one free lesson a day, with unlimited use on subscription. Wikipedia lists 126 employees. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yousician)
- Funding: Series B of USD 28M (€23.1M) in April 2021, led by True Ventures, with Amazon Alexa Fund and MPL Ventures. Total about €35M by end of 2021. — [TechCrunch, Apr 2021](https://techcrunch.com/2021/04/28/yousician-series-b/); [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/yousician-the-worlds-leading-platform-for-learning-and-playing-music-announces-closing-of-28-million-series-b-round-301279380.html); [EU-Startups](https://www.eu-startups.com/2021/04/helsinki-based-yousician-lands-e23-1-million-to-reimagine-music-education/)
- Financials [DISCLOSED via Finnish registry aggregators]:
  - 2022: revenue about €43M, operating result about −€8.1M, net result about −€7.6M. — [Finder.fi](https://finder.fi/Sovellukset+ja+ohjelmistot/Yousician+Oy/Helsinki/yhteystiedot/2476700)
  - 2024: revenue €53.3M (+0.9%), operating loss about €0.87M (−1.6%). — [Asiakastieto](https://www.asiakastieto.fi/yritykset/fi/yousician-oy/23743640/taloustiedot); [Proff.fi](https://www.proff.fi/yrityksen/yousician-oy/helsinki/it-konsultointi-ja-neuvonta/2374364-0I03DC). Taloustutka shows a 2024 operating result of −€824k but a net profit of +€1.02M, a source conflict. — [Taloustutka](https://www.taloustutka.fi/konserni/2374364-0)
  - 2025: revenue about €53.0M (−0.7%), operating profit about €3.5M (6.5% margin), net result about €3.0M. — [Asiakastieto](https://www.asiakastieto.fi/yritykset/fi/yousician-oy/23743640/yleiskuva); [Vainu](https://vainu.io/company/yousician-oy-taloustiedot-ja-liikevaihto/524204/yritystiedot). Upbeat Studio gives €3.7M operating profit and 118 staff. — [Upbeat Studio](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)
  - The aggregator estimates are wildly wrong: GetLatka says USD 3.6M revenue and an USD 18M valuation, and Tracxn says €50–100M. Use the registry figures. — [GetLatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/yousician); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/yousician/__9_js14rTh79nrKlExWy_o-y8h6uzWU-JoYbthACpUYg)
- GuitarTuna has 180M downloads (self-reported, 2026) and serves as the free funnel into paid Yousician. — [Upbeat Studio, citing guitartuna.com/about](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)
- Pricing (third-party, 2026): about USD 119.99/year for one instrument and USD 139–180/year for Premium+ (all instruments). One subscription site says "from USD 19.99/mo". — [Skoove comparison](https://www.skoove.com/blog/best-piano-apps/); [Subger](https://subger.com/en/service/yousician)
- AI and tech: patented real-time audio recognition. GuitarTuna "Play" (April 2022) uses AI to auto-scroll chord sheets. A chat-based AI guitar coach called "June" appears in a third-party review from about 2026 (unverified). The vendor Maxim AI says Yousician shipped 60 prompt versions in one month, which points to an LLM product in development. — [Guitar World, Apr 2022](https://www.guitarworld.com/news/yousician-guitartuna-play); [agent-finder review](https://agent-finder.co/reviews/ai-guitar-lessons-by-yousician-june-); [Maxim AI](https://www.getmaxim.ai/blog/building-the-future-of-music-education-yousicians-journey-with-maxim-ai/)
- Licensed content: in 2018 Yousician removed user-uploaded songs and moved to licensed "popular songs", which drew user backlash. It has artist courses such as Metallica, built on original master tracks. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yousician); [PR Newswire (Metallica)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/yousician-launches-three-beginner-friendly-courses-inviting-fans-to-learn-to-play-alongside-the-multi-grammy-award-winning-band-301567826.html)
- No Yousician acquisitions were found (Tracxn says none). — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/yousician/__9_js14rTh79nrKlExWy_o-y8h6uzWU-JoYbthACpUYg)

### Inferences
- Yousician gives the clearest unit-economics anchor in the category: about €53M of revenue from roughly 120 staff, around €450k revenue per employee. Getting to profitability took cost discipline rather than growth, because revenue has been flat since 2024. Investors can read this as "a mature incumbent, growth-stalled".

### Gaps
- No disclosed paid-subscriber count, MAU, or revenue split by instrument (piano share unknown). No official AI launch press release for 2025–2026 was found.

## 4. Other players: flowkey, Skoove, Playground Sessions, Piano Marvel, ONE Smart Piano, Pianote/Musora, Melodics, Trala, Pianu, Synthesia, Hello Music Theory

### Takeaway
Apart from Simply, Yousician and Musora, the field is small, founder-led or bootstrapped companies, mostly with less than USD 10M raised. The "acquired by Peaksoft/Yousician" premise for flowkey is **not supported**: flowkey is still an independent Berlin GmbH. The one confirmed small deal is **Skoove's parent Learnfield acquiring Trala** (violin). Almost every app runs a USD 100–200/year subscription with a curated, licensed song library.

### Cited Findings
**flowkey (Berlin)**
- flowkey GmbH was founded by Jonas Gössling (CEO), Alexander Heesing and Ahmed Hassan, and launched in 2014. It claims more than 15M registered users. Founders reportedly chose not to take VC; Crunchbase and Tracxn show only small angel or seed rounds. No sale was found. — [flowkey About](https://www.flowkey.com/en/about); [Startbase interview](https://www.startbase.com/interviews/je-aelter-ich-werde-umso-mehr-begeistert-mich-bach/); [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/flowkey); [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/99429-58)
- Uses German small-company filing rules to omit revenue (North Data, 2026). Yamaha bundles it, with the promotion running to March 2027. — [Upbeat Studio](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)
- Pricing (US, 2026, third-party): Classic USD 16.49/month or USD 99.99/year, which **excludes the licensed pop and soundtrack catalogue**. Premium USD 24.99/month or USD 149.99/year. Family USD 224.99/year. — [pract.is comparison](https://pract.is/blog/simply-piano-vs-flowkey-vs-piano-marvel-vs-yousician-2026-honest-comparison)
- Catalogue: about 1,000–1,500 songs. Reviews report licensed pop songs being removed. — [Piano Dreamers review](https://www.pianodreamers.com/flowkey-review/); [La Touche Musicale](https://latouchemusicale.com/en/comparaisons/flowkey-vs-simply-piano/)

**Skoove (Learnfield GmbH, Berlin)**
- Founded in 2014 by Florian Plenge and Stephan Schulz. Seed from High-Tech Gründerfonds (2015), €1.2M (2017; IBB Ventures, EGORA) and €2.9M (2019; Ringier Digital Ventures, MGO, caparuca), plus an EU MILES grant. Offers AI note recognition with real-time feedback. Available in 7 languages. Described as Berlin's #2 piano app after flowkey. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skoove)
- Pricing: about USD 29.99/month, USD 59.99/quarter, USD 149.99/year, with a 7-day trial (third-party, 2026). — [Skoove comparison via search](https://www.skoove.com/blog/best-piano-apps/)
- **M&A:** the App Store listing says the Trala apps "have been acquired by Learnfield GmbH, the company behind Skoove". No date is given. — [App Store: Trala](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/trala-learn-violin/id1143205265)

**Trala (Chicago, violin)**
- Raised USD 6.9M in total per Built In Chicago; Tracxn says USD 19.5M (conflict). Origin Ventures was an investor. Pricing is USD 29.99/month or USD 199.99/year. Now owned by Learnfield (see above). — [Built In Chicago](https://www.builtinchicago.org/articles/trala-raises-7m-funding); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/the-violin-app/__ArU1RYVSu22VnBDSRFz4PotqgTV-8qSrbre90xA-_9M); [Origin Ventures](https://www.originventures.com/blog/the-best-violin-teacher-you-never-had-origins-investment-in-trala/)

**Musora (Pianote, Drumeo, Guitareo, Singeo, PlayBass; Canada)**
- Has more than 100,000 paying subscribers, is profitable with "mid-eight-figure" revenue, and has declined buyout offers [REPORTED, Globe and Mail 2025]. All five brands sit under one membership, and the standalone Pianote app has been folded into the Musora app. It also publishes Beatlii, the renamed app of bankrupt Freedrum. — [Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-how-a-bc-drum-teacher-built-global-online-juggernaut-drumeo); [Upbeat Studio](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/); [Google Play: Musora](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.drumeo&hl=en_US)
- Funding: CB Insights says USD 3.62M in total, mostly government grants. Pacific Economic Development Canada reportedly committed C$5M in January 2026. — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/musora-media); [yespress profile](https://yespress.io/musora)
- Pianote pricing is about USD 197–200/year, the most expensive app in the comparisons. Its model is video lessons with human instructors. — [Skoove comparison](https://www.skoove.com/blog/best-piano-apps/); [Pianote About](https://www.pianote.com/about)

**Playground Sessions (US)**
- Formerly Zag Music Group, with Quincy Jones associated. PitchBook says USD 2.64M raised, with a secondary transaction in December 2022 and no sale reported. ## 5. Consolidation and M&A in music edtech, 2022–2026

### Takeaway
Consolidation has come mainly from **Muse Group** (Ultimate Guitar, MuseScore, Audacity, StaffPad, and **Hal Leonard** in December 2023, backed by Francisco Partners until its June 2026 exit) and from PE roll-ups of guitar video platforms. Pure piano-app M&A has been tiny (Learnfield–Trala). The period also saw many shutdowns (TakeLessons, Rocksmith+, Finale, Freedrum). Neither Simply nor Yousician has made an acquisition.

### Cited Findings
- **Muse Group acquired Hal Leonard**, closing December 1, 2023 (announced December 4). Terms were not disclosed. The sellers included Seidler Equity Partners, and Francisco Partners backed the deal. Hal Leonard is the largest sheet-music and education publisher: more than 600 staff, about USD 250M in annual sales in 2019, Sheet Music Direct with more than 1.6M titles, Noteflight with more than 8M members, and ArrangeMe with more than 4M pre-cleared songs. — [Scoring Notes, Dec 2023](https://www.scoringnotes.com/news/muse-group-acquires-hal-leonard/); [Billboard, Dec 2023](https://www.billboard.com/business/publishing/hal-leonard-joins-muse-group-music-publishing-deals-1235545453/)
- Muse Group scale at the time of the deal (Dec 2023): Ultimate Guitar had 200M annual visitors, 53M app downloads and 30M accounts. MuseScore.com had 100M annual visitors and 13M accounts. MuseScore reports licences for more than 5M songs. Group goal: "music literacy for one billion people". — [Scoring Notes](https://www.scoringnotes.com/news/muse-group-acquires-hal-leonard/)
- Muse Group acquisition timeline: MuseScore (2017/18), Crescendo (2018), Audacity (2021), StaffPad (2021), Hal Leonard (2023). Headquartered in Limassol, Cyprus. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muse_Group); [Scoring Notes 2021](https://www.scoringnotes.com/news/muse-group-formed-to-support-musescore-ultimate-guitar-acquires-audacity/)
- Francisco Partners exited Muse Group in June 2026 (Scoring Notes). — [Upbeat Studio](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)
- Growth Catalyst Partners rolled up TrueFire and JamPlay (2019–20), ArtistWorks (2021) and Blayze (2025). — [Upbeat Studio](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)
- Learnfield (Skoove) acquired Trala (date not stated). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/trala-learn-violin/id1143205265)
- Musora took over the Freedrum app (renamed Beatlii) after Freedrum's bankruptcy in October 2024. — [Upbeat Studio](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)
- Exits and closures: Spotify sold Soundtrap back to its founders (2023). Finale was sunset in August 2024. TakeLessons (Microsoft) closed November 15, 2024. Ubisoft's Rocksmith+ studio closed in December 2024 and the service is in maintenance mode. — [Upbeat Studio](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)
- Simply: no acquisitions found. Yousician: none (Tracxn). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_(software_company)); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/yousician/__9_js14rTh79nrKlExWy_o-y8h6uzWU-JoYbthACpUYg)

### Inferences
- Muse Group now controls both a vast licensed catalogue (Hal Leonard, MuseScore's 5M-song licences) and huge free traffic (Ultimate Guitar, MuseScore). That makes it the most dangerous potential entrant into "learn any song", and also the most natural licensing partner or acquirer for an AI piano startup.
- No large VC rounds since 2021, plus many closures, suggests investor fatigue with music edtech after COVID. A new pitch needs to explain why AI changes the economics.

### Gaps
- Hal Leonard deal price and Muse Group revenue were not disclosed. Nothing found on whether Muse Group has launched a piano-learning product since the deal.

## 6. Unit economics signals

### Takeaway
The category runs on annual subscriptions at about USD 100–200, sold through free trials and heavy paid social acquisition. Education apps convert installs to trials below the all-app median, but annual plans retain much better than monthly. Margins are thin: Yousician makes about 6.5% operating margin at €53M, and Simply was unprofitable at USD 100M in 2020.

### Cited Findings
- Price points (2026, third-party): Simply Piano about USD 120–225/year, flowkey USD 99.99–149.99/year, Skoove USD 149.99/year, Yousician USD 119.99–180/year, Piano Marvel USD 129.99/year, Playground Sessions USD 108–216/year, Pianote about USD 197–200/year, Trala USD 199.99/year, Synthesia USD 29–39 one-off. — sources in sections 2–4; [Skoove comparison](https://www.skoove.com/blog/best-piano-apps/); [pract.is](https://pract.is/blog/simply-piano-vs-flowkey-vs-piano-marvel-vs-yousician-2026-honest-comparison)
- RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2025: more than 80% of Education and Health & Fitness apps run trials of 5–9 days or longer. — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025)
- RevenueCat 2026: Education download-to-trial rate is 6.5%, against a 9.1% median across all apps (top quartile above 16.2%). — [RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps); [RevenueCat blog](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026)
- Trial conversion (2025, via secondary write-ups): 17–32-day trials convert at about 42.5–45.7%, against 25.5% for trials under 4 days. Hard-paywall apps convert 10.7% of installs to paid by Day 35, against 2.1% for freemium. — [RocketShip HQ summary](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/revenuecat-state-of-subscription-apps-2025-summary/); [Subscription Insider](https://www.subscriptioninsider.com/article-type/news/revenuecats-state-of-subscription-apps-2025-report-ais-dominance-retention-challenges-and-the-shift-away-from-pure-subscriptions)
- Retention (2025): weekly plans rarely exceed 10% retention at 6 months. Yearly plans can exceed 50–60% at 12 months, against about 20–40% for monthly. — [RocketShip HQ summary](https://www.rocketshiphq.com/revenuecat-state-of-subscription-apps-2025-summary/)
- Simply growth tactics: localized pricing tests (+15% revenue from one test) and hundreds of experiments. — [Medium / hellosimply](https://medium.com/hellosimply/how-big-macs-increased-our-revenue-by-15-71855cda055a)
- Paid UA reliance: Simply hires performance video creators for "large-scale video ads". Apple Search Ads is a documented channel. — [BuiltIn](https://builtin.com/job/marketing-video-creator/3795861); [Apple Ads](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/success-stories/joytunes)
- Free-funnel model: Yousician uses GuitarTuna (180M downloads). Duolingo offers its music course free. — [Upbeat Studio](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)
- COVID volatility: Simply's revenue rose 150% in 2020. Fender Play paid subscribers went from about 110k to a peak of about 930k during the 2020 promotion. — [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3910571,00.html); [Upbeat Studio citing Forbes](https://upbeat.studio/blog/the-online-music-learning-landscape/)
- Profitability: Yousician went from −€8.1M operating result (2022) to about +€3.5M (2025, 6.5% margin). Simply was "not profitable" in 2020 per Calcalist. Musora is reportedly profitable. — [Finder.fi](https://finder.fi/Sovellukset+ja+ohjelmistot/Yousician+Oy/Helsinki/yhteystiedot/2476700); [Asiakastieto](https://www.asiakastieto.fi/yritykset/fi/yousician-oy/23743640/yleiskuva); [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3910571,00.html)

### Inferences
- Implied ARPU: Simply in 2019 had about USD 20M from more than 200k payers, roughly USD 100/payer/year. That is consistent with discounted annual plans.
- Thin margins at €53M of revenue (Yousician) suggest UA plus content-licensing costs consume most gross profit. AI-driven content generation that lowers licensing or production cost, or organic acquisition, is the lever a startup can pitch.

### Gaps
- No music-learning-specific churn, trial-to-paid or CAC/LTV figures were published by any incumbent. No public Meta/TikTok spend estimates (Sensor Tower Pathmatics or AppMagic) for Simply or Yousician were found.

## 7. What incumbents do NOT do (positioning gaps)

### Takeaway
Incumbents teach from **curated, finite, licensed catalogues**, in the low thousands of songs, sometimes locked behind higher tiers or removed when licences lapse. Courses are produced in-house, and feedback is mostly note and timing correctness. None let a user learn *any* song they choose with adaptive arrangement. The exceptions are Synthesia (via MIDI files the user supplies) and sheet-music platforms such as MuseScore and Hal Leonard, which have huge catalogues but little pedagogy.

### Cited Findings
- flowkey's library is about 1,000–1,500 songs. Its cheaper Classic tier excludes licensed pop and soundtrack songs, and reviewers report licensed pop songs being removed. — [pract.is](https://pract.is/blog/simply-piano-vs-flowkey-vs-piano-marvel-vs-yousician-2026-honest-comparison); [Piano Dreamers](https://www.pianodreamers.com/flowkey-review/)
- Yousician removed user-uploaded songs in 2018 in favour of licensed songs, drawing user opposition. Its lessons are produced in-house. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yousician)
- Simply Piano songs sit inside its structured courses rather than an open library. It adds content through partnerships (Trinity College London 2024, Oxford University Press 2026). — [La Touche Musicale](https://latouchemusicale.com/en/comparaisons/flowkey-vs-simply-piano/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_(software_company)); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/joytunes)
- Synthesia ships with 150 songs and relies on user-supplied MIDI for everything else, with no structured pedagogy or licensing. — [Synthesia](https://www.synthesiagame.com/)
- Contrast: MuseScore reports licences for more than 5M songs, and Hal Leonard's ArrangeMe has pre-cleared more than 4M songs. Licensing at scale is possible, but only Muse Group has it, and it is not a guided piano tutor. — [Scoring Notes](https://www.scoringnotes.com/news/muse-group-acquires-hal-leonard/)
- AI features so far are incremental: Simply Sing's pitch feedback, Simply Draw's drawing feedback, Yousician's auto-scroll and reported chat coach. — [Wikipedia (Simply)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_(software_company)); [Guitar World](https://www.guitarworld.com/news/yousician-guitartuna-play); [agent-finder](https://agent-finder.co/reviews/ai-guitar-lessons-by-yousician-june-)

### Inferences
- The clearest wedge is "learn any song you want, arranged to your level, with real-time AI feedback". The licensing problem that limits incumbents is the main risk for a startup too: generating arrangements of copyrighted compositions still needs publisher (mechanical/print) licences. Muse Group/Hal Leonard is a likely licensing partner.
- A second wedge is pedagogy beyond correct notes, such as posture, technique and practice planning via an LLM coach. Incumbents only began exploring this in 2025–26.

### Gaps
- No primary-source documentation of incumbents' licensing contracts (publishers, terms, per-stream or per-print costs) was found. No user-survey data quantifying "can't find the song I want" as a churn reason was found.
