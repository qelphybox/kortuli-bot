# Anything Piano and the "learn ANY song on piano via AI" look-alikes (as of 19 Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-19. App Store figures are snapshots from that date unless marked otherwise. "Self-reported" = the vendor's own claim; "independent" = third-party or store data.

**Correction to the brief:** Songscription is not an outside competitor of Anything Piano. It is the same company: Songscription Inc., which now trades under the parent brand "Play Anything". So every Songscription blog comparison is first-party marketing for Anything Piano, not an independent view.

## 1. Anything Piano: company, launch, App Store listing, pricing, features, roadmap, funding, traction, marketing

### Takeaway
Anything Piano is an iPhone/iPad app from Songscription Inc. ("Play Anything"). The company was started by four Stanford-connected co-founders, incorporated in Dec 2024, and raised a $5M seed round led by Reach Capital in Nov 2025. The app's first App Store version is dated Jul 10 (2026). The app is small but growing: 4.8★ from 139 US ratings, $24.99/month or $179.99/year, and an unstated number of free conversions. It produces piano-roll tutorials only, with listening via mic or MIDI. Android, web and sheet music are promised but have no dates.

## 2. What users praise and complain about

### Takeaway
Feedback is thin: about 139 ratings and 4 visible US reviews, with no Reddit or TikTok discussion found. Reviewers praise transcription accuracy (including obscure tracks and jazz) and the simple UI. They complain about missed notes, paywalled songs, and no way to edit the generated notes.

## 3. Look-alike apps: what they do, pricing, platform, scale, differentiator

### Takeaway
Very few apps truly cover "arbitrary song → playable tutorial → listens to you" end to end. Anything Piano and Songscription (same company) are the only ones found doing all of it with full-mix arrangement. The nearest rivals each do part of the job:
- **Falling Note** (iOS, Mar 2026): piano *videos* → waterfall.
- **ZumaPiano** (Chrome, Android, iOS): browser audio from piano songs → waterfall, cheap.
- **Synthesia, PianoVision, La Touche Musicale, Artie:** import MIDI/your own song, but need a MIDI file.
- **Piano2Notes / PianoConvert:** transcription only.
- **Moises:** stems and chords, not tutorials.

Catalog incumbents (Simply Piano, flowkey, Skoove, Yousician, Playground Sessions, Pianu) do not accept arbitrary songs.

## 4. How the apps handle the arrangement problem (raw transcription vs simplified playable arrangement)

### Takeaway
There are three approaches:
1. **Raw transcription** of piano audio (Piano2Notes, PianoConvert, Falling Note, ZumaPiano, LumaKeys). Faithful, but unplayable for beginners and limited to piano recordings.
2. **AI arrangement** of a full mix into a solo-piano part (Anything Piano, Songscription). Songscription offers explicit difficulty levels.
3. **Human-arranged catalogs** with multiple levels (flowkey, Simply Piano, Artie, PianoVision Plus).

Anything Piano currently gives two modes: faithful 1:1 when the audio is piano-only, and a "sounds like the original" arrangement otherwise. Its "easy/medium/hard" setting (since v1.0.10) is tied to mastery mode, and the company has not said whether it actually simplifies the notes.

### Cited Findings
- Anything Piano asks before conversion whether the audio contains piano.
  - Piano-only audio: it outputs exactly what the piano plays and "ignores everything else."
  - Regular song: it "combines the melody, vocals, and other instruments into one piano arrangement."
  - Source: [Help](https://playanything.com/help/how-anything-piano-makes-tutorials-for-any-song)
- Landing-page FAQ: piano-only recordings produce a roll that "mirrors the recording 1:1". Mixed recordings produce a piano roll that "sounds like the original." — [playanything.com/anythingpiano](https://playanything.com/anythingpiano)
- Music Ally / AI Musicpreneur describes the output as a "simplified piano tutorial with guided fingerings." — [AI Musicpreneur](https://www.aimusicpreneur.com/ai-tools-news/songscription-publisher-licensing-deals-play-anything-rebrand/)
- v1.0.10 added "three difficulties: easy, medium, and hard" alongside mastery-mode scoring. The release notes don't say whether difficulty changes the notes or only how strictly playing is scored. The help pages don't mention difficulty levels at all. — [App Store version history](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/anything-piano-learn-any-song/id6761146933?see-all=version-history), [Help](https://playanything.com/help/does-anything-piano-show-sheet-music)
- Songscription (sister product) generates piano covers at **5 levels**. Each level is a new arrangement, generated in about 30 seconds.
  - Adaptive (default): closest to the recording.
  - Beginner–intermediate: simpler textures, fewer notes per hand.
  - Intermediate.
  - Intermediate–advanced: denser harmony.
  - Advanced: most detail.
  - It also offers piano accompaniment, lead sheet, piano + vocal, and a "sheet music leveler" for simplifying existing MusicXML.
  - Source: [Songscription FAQ](https://www.songscription.ai/faq)
- Songscription's piano model is its most mature. Accuracy is highest on clean single-instrument recordings, and busy mixes may need cleanup. — [Songscription FAQ](https://www.songscription.ai/faq)
- Raw-transcription look-alikes:
  - PianoConvert: input should be piano-only without vocals. — [La Touche Musicale help](https://latouchemusicale.crunch.help/en/english/piano-convert-how-does-it-work)
  - Falling Note: input is piano *video*. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6760156164)
  - ZumaPiano: "Turn any piano song into practice." — [zumapiano.ai](https://zumapiano.ai/)
  - LumaKeys: transcribes audio into editable notes, so the user fixes or simplifies by hand. — [LumaKeys](https://lumakeys.app/anything-piano-alternative)
- Human-arranged multi-level catalogs:
  - flowkey: "multiple difficulty levels per song". — [Songscription blog](https://www.songscription.ai/blog/best-piano-learning-apps)
  - Artie: Easy/Medium/Hard. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6746756746)

### Inferences
- The defensible technical moat is generating a *playable* arrangement from a full mix, at several difficulty levels, with consistent hand and finger assignment. Play Anything already has a 5-level arranger in Songscription and is likely to carry it into Anything Piano. That is not confirmed.
- Editing is the main gap. Since AI output can drop notes or assign the wrong hand, the absence of an in-app editor (Anything Piano) pushes power users toward tools like LumaKeys or Falling Note, which allow note editing.

### Gaps
- No independent accuracy benchmarks for any of these apps.
- Unknown whether Anything Piano's easy/medium/hard setting changes the note content.
