# Songscription AI (songscription.ai) / Play Anything: competitor profile as of October 2026

Research date: 2026-10-19. "Self-reported" marks claims that come only from the company or its investors.

**Main finding:** by October 2026 Songscription is no longer a single product. The company now presents itself as **"Play Anything"**, a "music AI lab" (playanything.com). It runs two products:
1. **Songscription**, a web tool that turns audio into notation.
2. **Anything Piano**, an iOS app for learning piano with real-time feedback. It competes directly with Simply Piano, Flowkey and Skoove.

---

## 1. Product: what it does, inputs and outputs, instruments, editing, playback, learning, platforms

### Takeaway
Songscription is a web tool (browser only). It turns audio, video or a YouTube/Instagram/TikTok link into sheet music, a piano roll, guitar tabs or MIDI. It has an editor, an AI edit assistant, arrangement modes and a Multi-Instrument mode (beta). Learning features sit in a separate iOS app, Anything Piano.

### Cited Findings
**Songscription (web): inputs**
- Accepts MP3, WAV and M4A uploads; YouTube, Instagram and TikTok links; and recording in the browser — [songscription.ai](https://www.songscription.ai/)
- At launch (June 2025) the inputs were audio files and YouTube links. Uploading requires ticking a box confirming you hold the rights to the audio — [TechCrunch, 2025-06-30](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/songscription-launches-an-ai-powered-shazam-for-sheet-music)

**Outputs and exports**
- Outputs: sheet music, piano roll and guitar tabs. Exports: PDF, MIDI, MusicXML (for MuseScore and Sibelius) and Guitar Pro. Tabs support standard tuning only — [songscription.ai](https://www.songscription.ai/)
- Exports require Plus or Pro. The free plan allows playback and editing in the browser only — [Play Anything blog, Oct 2026](https://playanything.com/blog/state-of-the-art-music-transcription)

**Instruments**
- Single-instrument models (Oct 2026): piano, acoustic guitar, bass, violin, flute, trumpet, saxophone, drums and vocals — [songscription.ai](https://www.songscription.ai/)
- How the list grew:
  - June 2025: piano, violin, flute, guitar ([Reach Capital, 2025-06-27](https://www.reachcapital.com/?p=6093))
  - November 2025: added bass guitar and trumpet ([MBW, 2025-11-13](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/songscription-raises-5m-in-funding-as-shazam-for-sheet-music-platform-reaches-150k-users/))
- Piano was the most reliable model at launch — [TechCrunch, 2025-06-30](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/songscription-launches-an-ai-powered-shazam-for-sheet-music)

**Multi-Instrument mode (beta)**
- Transcribes a full mix with a separate part per instrument: keys, guitars, bass, strings, brass, woodwinds, saxophones, synths, voice and drums. The FAQ admits dense mixes need manual cleanup — [songscription.ai](https://www.songscription.ai/); [seed blog post, updated 2026-10-07](https://www.songscription.ai/blog/seed-funding-announcement)

**Arrangements**
- Piano cover at a chosen difficulty, piano + vocal arrangement, and lead sheet with chord symbols — [songscription.ai](https://www.songscription.ai/)

**Editor and playback**
- Built-in notation editor
- "Annie", an AI assistant that makes edits from plain-language requests
- Automatic piano fingering
- Playback with adjustable speed
- Interactive piano roll with left/right hand split, transposition and colours, aimed at people who don't read notation
- Guitar tabs show the finger for each note
- Source: [songscription.ai](https://www.songscription.ai/)

**Other surfaces**
- Free SEO tools: Audio to Sheet Music, YouTube to Sheet Music, MP3 to MIDI, MIDI to Sheet Music
- A Sheet Music Library at songscription.ai/library
- Source: [playanything.com](https://playanything.com/)

**Anything Piano (iOS): the learning product**
- Platforms: iPhone and iPad only. Android and web have a waitlist — [playanything.com/anythingpiano](https://playanything.com/anythingpiano)
- Song import: audio or MIDI files, YouTube/TikTok/Instagram links, a live recording, or a free Public Library. The AI builds a piano roll (falling notes) — [playanything.com/anythingpiano](https://playanything.com/anythingpiano)
- Practice features — [playanything.com/anythingpiano](https://playanything.com/anythingpiano):
  - Real-time feedback on right and wrong notes
  - Wait mode that holds the song until the correct note is played
  - One-hand or two-hand practice, loops and slow-down
  - Finger numbers, note names and chords
  - Songs split into parts
- Input: microphone (acoustic or digital piano) or a MIDI keyboard over Bluetooth or USB — [playanything.com/anythingpiano](https://playanything.com/anythingpiano)
- No sheet music yet, only a piano roll; notation is "in development". No technique or scale drills — [blog "best piano learning apps", 2026-08-30](https://www.songscription.ai/blog/best-piano-learning-apps)
- Roadmap in the MIDI Innovation Awards entry: Anything Guitar and Anything Drums, Android, feedback on timing and dynamics, light-up MIDI keyboards, sheet music generation — [midi.org entry, 2026](https://midi.org/?p=7621)
- Product Hunt: tagline "Learn any song on piano". Makers: Katie Baker, Andrew Carlins, Alex Alvarado. 11 upvotes, no reviews. Launch date unclear: the page says "Launched 2mo ago", but the leaderboard link points to 2026-10-19 — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/anything-piano)

### Inferences
- The split is clear. Songscription (web) serves notation and production users: transcribers, teachers, composers, producers. Anything Piano (iOS) serves the consumer learning market.
- Anything Piano's core pitch is "any song, not a catalog". It is aimed squarely at catalog apps (Simply Piano, Flowkey, Skoove) and at Synthesia.

### Gaps
- I found no exact Anything Piano launch date on the App Store, and could not open the App Store listing itself.
- Songscription has no native mobile app of its own; the mobile product is Anything Piano.

## 2. Pricing and B2B/API offering

### Takeaway
The pricing page (October 2026) has four tiers: Free, Plus, Pro and Enterprise. Paid tiers are credit-based. Prices on the page load dynamically and were not visible when I fetched it. The last publicly reported prices (2025) were Plus $9.99/month and Pro $29.99/month. API access exists only in Enterprise, with custom pricing.

### Cited Findings
- **Free ($0):** 30-second transcriptions, no exports. A 14-day trial with no card required gives 2-minute transcriptions — [pricing page, Oct 2026](https://www.songscription.ai/pricing)
- **Plus:** transcriptions up to 15 minutes; 600 credits/month (about 60 minutes); MIDI, MusicXML, PDF and Guitar Pro export; opt-out from AI training — [pricing](https://www.songscription.ai/pricing)
- **Pro:** up to 15 minutes; 3,000 credits/month (about 300 minutes); same exports; priority support; opt-out from training — [pricing](https://www.songscription.ai/pricing)
- **Enterprise:** "Contact sales"; API access, custom limits, bulk transcription, dedicated support, invoice payment; aimed at studios and professional teams — [pricing](https://www.songscription.ai/pricing)
- **One-time unlock:** trial users can pay once to unlock a single transcription — [songscription.ai](https://www.songscription.ai/)
- **Referrals:** 5 referred friends earn a free month of Plus — [pricing](https://www.songscription.ai/pricing)
- **2025 prices (dated):**
  - MusicRadar (dated 12/08/2025 on the clip page; the format is ambiguous): Free gave up to 10 transcriptions of 3 minutes per month; Plus was $9.99/month for 5 transcriptions of 6 minutes per month; Pro was $29.99/month for up to 100 transcriptions of 15 minutes per month — [MusicRadar via StoryAmp](https://songscription.rockpaperscissors.biz/artist/21268/press_clips/33364/white)
  - RouteNote (2025, via search snippet): Pro $29.99/month; free tier gave unlimited 30-second transcriptions plus 10 three-minute ones per month — [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/ai-startup-transcribes-audio-tracks-to-sheet-music-in-minutes/)
- **Anything Piano pricing:** free to download, a limited number of free song conversions, and a Pro subscription for unlimited songs. No price is published on the site — [playanything.com/anythingpiano](https://playanything.com/anythingpiano); [midi.org](https://midi.org/?p=7621)
- **Anything Piano trial:** Product Hunt makers offered one free month — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/anything-piano)

### Inferences
- The free tier tightened sharply from 2025 to 2026. In 2025 it gave ten 3-minute transcriptions a month; in 2026 it gives 30-second clips and no exports. This suggests a push towards monetisation.
- The model moved from counting transcriptions to counting minutes (credits).
- A blog post titled "Transcription software for music schools" (Oct 2026) points to interest in the education/B2B segment — [blog index](https://www.songscription.ai/blog)

### Gaps
- Current (October 2026) Plus and Pro prices, monthly or annual, could not be retrieved: they render on the client side.
- I found no public API documentation, API prices or named B2B/API customers.

## 5. Technology: models, data, accuracy, licensing, awards

### Takeaway
The models are proprietary, built on Tim Beyer's end-to-end architecture (ISMIR 2024). The data is mostly synthetic, plus public domain material and artist partnerships; publisher deals are "under negotiation". In October 2026 the company published its own benchmark claiming state of the art against Klang.io, Mirelo, YourMT3+, Basic Pitch and others. Anything Piano is only an entrant in the MIDI Innovation Awards; I found no evidence of a win.

### Cited Findings
- **Most training data is synthetic.** Sheet music is rendered to audio, then noise and reverb are added. On top of that: public domain scores and performances sold or shared by musicians — [TechCrunch, 2025-06-30](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/songscription-launches-an-ai-powered-shazam-for-sheet-music)
- **Training data and licensing (November 2025):** public domain plus partnerships with individual artists and music companies. The company says it is negotiating with global music publishers. Advisor Elizabeth Moody says it is working on deals with major publishers and that "ethics are top of mind" — [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/songscription-raises-5m-in-funding-as-shazam-for-sheet-music-platform-reaches-150k-users/); [company blog](https://www.songscription.ai/blog/seed-funding-announcement)
- **Paid users can opt out of their uploads being used to train the AI** — [pricing](https://www.songscription.ai/pricing)
- **Own benchmark (October 2026, self-reported, published by the "Play Anything Engineering Team")** — [playanything.com/blog/state-of-the-art-music-transcription](https://playanything.com/blog/state-of-the-art-music-transcription):
  - MIDI accuracy, averaged across genres (250 songs): Songscription 59.5, Klang.io 50.6, Mirelo 49.7, MuScriptor 46.6, YourMT3+ 46.3, Basic Pitch 35.8
  - Piano to MIDI (ASAP, 50 classical pieces): Songscription 89.4, Klang.io 88.9, YourMT3+ 70.2. Klang.io is almost level on piano MIDI.
  - Multi-instrument to MIDI (200 tracks from RWC, JazzSAMBA, MulTTiPop): Songscription 52.2, Mirelo 45.1
  - Piano to sheet music: 58.5 vs 49.3 for Klang.io
  - Multi-instrument to sheet music (28 tracks from a private test set): 61.1 vs 46.5 for Klang.io
  - Metrics: mir_eval onset, onset-offset and frame F1 for MIDI; MUSTER and OMR-NED for sheet music
  - Arrangement, blind votes (100 per comparison): beats Pop2Piano (97%), Etude (93%) and Klang.io Melody Scanner (80%). The headline percentages don't match the breakdowns (49/39/12 vs Melody Scanner), and the page doesn't explain the difference.
  - Methodological weaknesses: the private test set, and the "3 commercial + 5 open-source" count doesn't match the systems named
- **Anything Piano:** the team trains its own models for audio to piano MIDI and claims "state-of-the-art" models for predicting hand and finger — [midi.org](https://midi.org/?p=7621)
- **MIDI Association:** Anything Piano is listed as an entry in the MIDI Innovation Awards (2026, Commercial Software Products). The page shows no finalist or winner status — [midi.org/?p=7621](https://midi.org/?p=7621). I found no search results linking Songscription to winners — [midi.org/innovation-awards](https://www.midi.org/innovation-awards)

### Inferences
- The defensible core is the in-house model plus synthetic data. Their own benchmark shows a gap of only 0.5 points to Klang.io on piano MIDI. The advantage is mostly in multi-instrument and in notation (rendering MIDI into a readable score).
- The licensing question is still open. A "rights checkbox" and "negotiations" with publishers, but no announced publisher deals.

### Gaps
- I found no confirmed signed licensing deals with publishers as of October 2026.
- No independent (non-company) benchmark found.
- MIDI Innovation Awards 2026 result (finalist or winner) not confirmed. The "innovation award" in the task brief may refer to this entry; I could not verify it.

## 7. User reviews and complaints

### Takeaway
There is little independent feedback. I found no Reddit or Trustpilot threads in search results. The most detailed independent review, MusicRadar (2025), is critical: pitch detection is good, but rhythm and time signatures are often wrong, output needs heavy cleanup, and multi-instrument tracks are weak. A third-party review from 2026 also says output should be treated as a "reconstruction that needs verification".

### Cited Findings
- **MusicRadar (2025), reviewer is a teacher of a transcription class at NYU** — [MusicRadar via StoryAmp](https://songscription.rockpaperscissors.biz/artist/21268/press_clips/33364/white):
  - Pros: very accurate pitch detection, including grace notes and complex chords; a freemium model
  - Cons: uneven rhythm detection; often incorrect or "nonsensical" time signatures; charts need a lot of formatting; struggles with multi-instrument tracks
  - Verdict: works best on beginner classical piano and simple solo piano pop. "It's helpful in situations where you don't need help, and unhelpful in situations where you do need help." A "remarkable tech demo"; headline: "Humans will be doing all the serious music transcription for the foreseeable future"
- **Jack Righteous review (2026, August per search summary):** strongest as a bridge from AI-generated audio to editable parts; dense AI mixes are unproven; any exported score or MIDI "should be treated as a reconstruction that needs verification" — [Jack Righteous](https://jackrighteous.com/de-gb/blogs/ai-creator-tools/songscription-review-2026-ai-music-sheet-music-midi). I could not fetch the full page (HTTP 429), so this rests on the search snippet.
- **RouteNote** called the rights verification a "checkbox system" — [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/ai-startup-transcribes-audio-tracks-to-sheet-music-in-minutes/)
- **The company itself** admits dense mixes need manual cleanup and that accuracy depends heavily on recording quality — [songscription.ai](https://www.songscription.ai/); [blog: accurate AI transcriptions](https://www.songscription.ai/blog/accurate-ai-music-transcriptions)
- **Product Hunt (Anything Piano):** no reviews — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/anything-piano)

### Inferences
- Likely pain points: rhythm and quantisation, time signatures, polyphony and mixes, and a 2026 free tier that got much tighter (30 seconds, no export). That last one is a likely source of pricing complaints, but I found no direct complaints to confirm it.

### Gaps
- Reddit (r/piano, r/musictheory), Trustpilot and App Store reviews: nothing relevant in search results; I did not search the sites directly.
- I found no complaints about billing or subscriptions.

## 8. Strategic direction: learning vs transcription

### Takeaway
They are clearly moving into learning. Funding coverage (November 2025) already named a "learning app with live feedback / music coach in your pocket" as the goal. By August–October 2026 there is a separate iOS app, Anything Piano, an umbrella brand (Play Anything) and SEO content against Simply Piano and its peers. Transcription stays as the technology core and the B2B/pro product (Enterprise/API, Multi-Instrument).

### Cited Findings
- **November 2025 roadmap:** more instruments; automatic levelling and arrangement to the user's skill; real-time feedback as a "music coach in your pocket" — [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/songscription-raises-5m-in-funding-as-shazam-for-sheet-music-platform-reaches-150k-users/); [blog](https://www.songscription.ai/blog/seed-funding-announcement)
- **Reach Capital, an EdTech VC, frames the product as a learning tool for teachers and self-driven learners** — [Reach Capital](https://www.reachcapital.com/?p=6093)
- **Play Anything ("Enabling anyone to play anything")** brings together Songscription, Anything Piano and the Sheet Music Library, with audience pages for Learners, Teachers, Performers, Composers, Producers and Transcribers. The site is in 9 languages — [playanything.com](https://playanything.com/)
- **Songscription's homepage carries a banner for the mobile app for piano learners** that links to playanything.com — [songscription.ai](https://www.songscription.ai/)
- **Anything Piano's planned expansion:** Anything Guitar and Anything Drums, timing and dynamics feedback, sheet music — [midi.org](https://midi.org/?p=7621)
- **CEO's 2025 vision:** a rural band teacher gets arrangements matched to each student's level — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/songscription-launches-an-ai-powered-shazam-for-sheet-music)

### Inferences
- **Competitive threat to a Simply Piano–style product:** high for the "learn any song" segment. Their transcription and arrangement stack (difficulty levelling, fingering, hand split) is exactly what catalog apps lack.
- **Weaknesses in learning, as of October 2026:**
  - iOS only
  - Piano roll with no notation
  - No structured curriculum or technique teaching
  - A small rating base
  - Feedback only on correct notes (timing and dynamics are on the roadmap)
  - Copyright risk around user uploads from YouTube/TikTok with no publisher licences
- **Overall weaknesses:**
  - The traction metrics are loose and self-reported
  - The accuracy benchmark is their own
  - Independent reviewers are sceptical about rhythm and polyphony
  - The free tier is getting tighter
  - $5M is modest for fighting both consumer EdTech (Simply Piano, flowkey) and transcription tools (Klang.io) at once

### Gaps
- Whether the company has formally renamed itself "Play Anything" (legal entity): the site footer still says "© 2026 Songscription" — [playanything.com](https://playanything.com/)
